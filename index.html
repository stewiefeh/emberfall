Emberfall

carry the light · bank the embers · come home

How to play

The torch burns constantly. It is your light, your clock, and your shield. When it dies, you are prey.

Shades circle the edge of your light. In the light they burn. Brush one while lit and it dies, but the flame pays fuel for it.

Embers drift in the dark. The further they sit from camp, the more they are worth. The pull of the fire draws them to you.

Nothing counts until it is banked. Carry embers home to the campfire. Die with them and the dark keeps them.

Every night is longer. Survive to dawn, choose a boon, and the dark comes back hungrier.

The night has moods. When the sky bleeds, your flame shrinks. Blue wisps lead to buried caches. At the fire, press E to stoke it with embers. Every third night the mother rises: only the camp's light truly burns her.

WASD or arrows to move · Space to dash · E to stoke · Esc to pause · M to mute
